BUY AND SELL RULES OF THE
STRATEGY
BUY RULES
1. Prices should close above the VWAP.
2. ADX should be below the 30 level.
3. RSI should cross and close above the 55 level.
SELL RULES
1. Prices should close below the VWAP.
2. ADX should below the 30 level.
3. RSI should cross and close below the 35 level.
BUY EXIT RULES
1. Prices should close below the VWAP or after the 3.00 the buy
position will automatically get squared off.
SELL EXIT RULES
1. Prices should close above the VWAP or after the 3.00 the sell
position will automatically get squared off.
(NOTE – I have added one more condition that the buy and sell trades
should happen between 09.15  & 14.30 )
