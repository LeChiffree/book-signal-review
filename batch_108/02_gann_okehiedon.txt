The okehiedon Gann System is based on the
principle, buy low and sell high off the 4hr
Chart and also incorporates some form of
trend trading by buying the dips in an uptrend
and selling the rallies in a downtrend based
on a 4hr, 30/15 min MTF strategy

4 HR SWING SYSTEM
Buy Signal
1. Price at support areas(Pivot Points, former
significant support etc)
2. OBOS gives buy signal
3. Enter when price closes above gann 5
4. Stop loss – 10 pips below swing low
5. Take profit –First Target at Risk. Move stop to
Break Even and Target major resistance or price
closes below Gann or OBOS gives sell signal
www.forexgercekleri.xyz - [Twitter: @eseckal]


=== PDF PAGE 12 ===
4 HR SWING SYSTEM
Sell Signal
1. Price at Resistance areas(Pivot Points, former
significant support etc)
2. OBOS gives Sell signal
3. Enter when price closes below gann 5
4. Stop loss – 10 pips above swing high
5. Take profit –First Target at risk, Move stop to
Break Even and Target major support or OBOS gives
buy signal or price closes above gann

MTF STRATEGY 4HR ,30 MIN CHART
Buy Signal
1. Price above Gann 5 on 4hr chart
2. Stoch not overbought
3. OBOS has not given sell signal.
4. Go to the 30 min chart using the same
template and buy all the dips in the direction of the
4hr chart as indicated by the gann 30 min system.
5. For aggressive entries you can buy the OBOS
signal with stoch supporting the trade in the
direction of the 4hr trend. Sell Signal is vice versa
